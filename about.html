The foundationOUR STORY

Rooted here.
Built for what's next.

Thrive Local Foundation is a Leavenworth, Kansas-based nonprofit focused on practical work that strengthens local businesses, organizations and opportunity.

What we believe

Communities get stronger
when useful things happen.

LISTEN LOCALLY

Listen before designing.

Understand a local need as it exists, rather than imposing a predetermined solution.

BUILD PRACTICALLY

Make the next step practical.

Use technology, research and organization to reduce real friction.

SHARE WHAT WORKS

Leave people more capable.

Document, hand off, and strengthen the ability of owners or organizations to do their work.

Founder / President

Jon Kesler

Jon founded Thrive Local Foundation around a belief that local businesses and young people deserve stronger opportunities. The organization's work has expanded through direct support, technical systems, community collaboration and independent research into local economic challenges.

The approach is practical: listen to people, understand the barrier, and help turn a promising idea into something a business or community organization can use.

Thrive's nonprofit work remains separate from the founder's independent businesses and public responsibilities. Research and project descriptions should be judged on their own evidence and work.
