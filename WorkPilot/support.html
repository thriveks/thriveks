Support for WorkPilot OS on iOS

Help, troubleshooting, and contact.

Find answers to common questions below. If you still need help, contact support and include details about what you were trying to do.

FAQ

  • How do I add a new location?
    Open Locations → tap + → enter the location details → tap Save.
  • Where do I log a service visit?
    Open a location → go to Visits → tap + → complete the form → Save.
  • How do I track expenses?
    Go to Expenses → tap + → enter the amount and details → Save.
  • Does WorkPilot OS require an account?
    WorkPilot OS is designed to be usable immediately. Just open the app and start adding locations, visits, and expenses.
  • My app icon didn’t update — what do I do?
    iOS can occasionally cache app icons. Try restarting your iPhone, or deleting and reinstalling the app. If it still doesn’t update, contact support.
When contacting support Include your device model and iOS version, plus a short description of what happened.
