Source Library

This library describes the official records used to build the Leavenworth County data-center public record. It distinguishes preserved primary documents from items referenced by officials but not located in the public postings reviewed through August 25, 2026.

Source hierarchy: primary documents and official votes → official meeting recordings → consultant analyses → attributed representations → public testimony. The site does not elevate a public-comment claim to verified fact unless another source supports it.
