Leadership

Built by local leadership with a long-term stake in Leavenworth.

Jonathan Kesler
President & Founder

Jonathan Kesler is a Leavenworth entrepreneur, operator, and civic-minded leader focused on economic expansion, enterprise creation, and long-range opportunity for working families. As President and Founder of Thrive Local Foundation, he leads efforts centered on business development, operational systems, and place-based revitalization.

His background spans nonprofit leadership, company-building, workflow design, transportation service, and hands-on operations. He is focused on strengthening the conditions that allow families, employers, and local enterprise to stay and grow in Leavenworth.
