Common Questions

The app is not opening the right ordinance section.

Make sure you are using the latest version of the app. If the issue continues, send a note to dev@thriveks.com with the section you searched and what happened.

The PDF source is slow to open.

The bundled ordinance PDF is a large file, so opening and jumping to a matching section may take a moment depending on device performance.

Does this app provide legal advice?

No. Code Companion is a reference tool only. Users should verify important matters against official ordinance sources and consult qualified legal or city staff when needed.
